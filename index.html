About Me

I am a Research Scientist at Megagon Labs in Mountain View, California. I hold a Ph.D. in Information Science and Technology from Osaka University. My research passion lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Graph Neural Networks, and Machine Learning.

Currently, I am focusing on making Large Language Models (LLMs) more reliable and capable through function calling, retrieval augmentation, and long-context understanding.
